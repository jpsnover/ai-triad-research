## Opening Statements

### Safetyist (safetyist)

Unchecked competition in AI, where a handful of entities control foundational models, will replicate this dynamic with far greater stakes, impacting the very stability of our social and political systems.

When a few private entities control the development of a technology with civilizational stakes, the necessary independent scrutiny and public accountability are systematically undermined.
