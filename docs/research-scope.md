The venues and topics the Collaborator scouts for the AI Triad Research project. Reference during a research sweep; keep it current as project priorities shift. (Extracted from the Collaborator role AGENTS.md to keep that file lean.)

## Primary Sources
- **ACL (Association for Computational Linguistics)** — https://www.aclweb.org/portal/
  - ACL Anthology (papers): https://aclanthology.org/
  - Conference proceedings (ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, CoNLL)
  - Special Interest Groups (SIGDIAL for dialogue, SIGGEN for generation)
- **arXiv** — cs.CL (Computation and Language), cs.AI (Artificial Intelligence), cs.MA (Multi-Agent Systems)

## Topics of Interest
Given what the project builds, prioritize research on:
- **Argumentation mining & frameworks** — AIF extensions, argument structure detection, claim extraction, attack/support classification
- **Multi-agent debate** — LLM debate protocols, deliberation frameworks, adversarial collaboration
- **Stance detection & POV analysis** — identifying and classifying viewpoints in text
- **Ontology-grounded NLP** — DOLCE, BFO, BDI in NLP pipelines; knowledge graph alignment
- **Taxonomy construction** — automated taxonomy building, concept hierarchy extraction
- **Fact verification & claim checking** — grounding claims in evidence, web-augmented verification
- **Dialogue systems** — multi-turn coherence, commitment tracking, consistency enforcement
- **Relevance theory in NLP** — Sperber & Wilson applied to context injection, information selection
- **Prompt engineering research** — systematic studies on prompt structure, context window optimization

## What's NOT Relevant
Pure machine translation, speech recognition, low-resource languages, or OCR — unless they directly apply to argument analysis or taxonomy work.
